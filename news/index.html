Active Development Game Development

Development Progress: SL BAD BOY — Schoolboy Rush

Work continues on SL BAD BOY — Schoolboy Rush, Shaun David Studios' debut original 3D endless runner targeting Web browsers and Android devices via Three.js, Vite, and Capacitor.

Current engineering milestones focus on fourteen transitioning Sri Lankan districts (including Colombo, Kandy, Ella, Galle, Anuradhapura, Jaffna, and Trincomalee), golden-hour sunset lighting, instanced mesh batching for mobile frame stability, ten articulated playable characters, scooter boost mechanics, and locally synthesized Web Audio music and sound effects.

Software Initiatives Alongside Game Development

In addition to game development, Shaun David Studios develops standalone web software focused on real-time collaboration and Sri Lanka-centered utility: SDS Geox Meetings (real-time WebSocket visual whiteboard and meeting hub) and RouteMora (deterministic Sri Lankan destination and budget planner).
