Colombo Fort approach, Galle Face horizon, standalone Colombo Lotus Tower, and evening bus traffic.

In Active Development Three.js · Vite · Capacitor

An Original 3D Sri Lankan Endless Runner

SL BAD BOY — Schoolboy Rush blends fast three-lane endless-runner action with original procedural Sri Lankan landmarks, Sinhala, Tamil, and English roadside signage, ten unlockable playable runners, a 1.2× scooter boost, mango-juice revives, and synthesized Web Audio music.

All 3D models, textures, signage, and audio cues are generated locally with adaptive quality scaling so the game runs smoothly across both mobile touchscreens and desktop browsers.
