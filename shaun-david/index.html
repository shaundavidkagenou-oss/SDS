Founder & CEO · Shaun David Studios

Shaun David

Founder & CEO of Shaun David Studios — an independent game developer and software creator based in Sri Lanka, focused on building original games, practical web software, and culturally grounded interactive experiences.

Biography & Creative Work

Shaun David founded Shaun David Studios in Sri Lanka as an independent creative and technical studio dedicated to original games, web applications, and digital audio craftsmanship. Serving as the studio's Founder and CEO, he oversees concept design, system architecture, gameplay programming, and product execution across every studio initiative.

Independent Game Development

A central focus of Shaun David's work is building accessible, responsive 3D games that work cleanly across both mobile devices and personal computers without relying on heavy external asset packs. His flagship game project at the studio, SL BAD BOY — Schoolboy Rush, is an original 3D endless runner developed with Three.js, JavaScript, Vite, and Capacitor for Web and Android.

Rather than using generic urban backdrops, he designed the game's world around custom procedural rendering, carefully budgeted draw calls, synthesized Web Audio music and sound effects, and responsive lane-and-gesture mechanics tailored for touchscreens as well as desktop keyboards.

Games Inspired by Sri Lankan Culture

Shaun David has a strong creative interest in bringing recognizable Sri Lankan places, atmosphere, and everyday character into interactive media. In SL BAD BOY — Schoolboy Rush, that vision takes shape across fourteen distinct districts — spanning Colombo, Kandy, Ella, Galle, Negombo, Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, Jaffna, Trincomalee, and surrounding towns.

By weaving together golden-hour coastal and hill-country lighting, architectural silhouettes inspired by local landmarks, public and private bus palettes, king-coconut stalls, tea-country viaducts, and trilingual Sinhala, Tamil, and English roadside signage, his work aims to make Sri Lankan settings feel vivid and natural inside modern gameplay.

Software Projects & Web Engineering

Alongside game development, Shaun David builds standalone software applications under the Shaun David Studios banner. His software work spans real-time WebSocket visual collaboration in SDS Geox Meetings, deterministic Sri Lankan destination and budget itinerary planning in RouteMora, and bilingual English-and-Sinhala interactive mathematics lessons in SD Education.

Across both games and software, his approach reflects the studio crest's guiding motto — Creating Music · Games · & Everything Beyond — combining disciplined software engineering with original creative identity.
